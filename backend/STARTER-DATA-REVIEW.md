## water

Water is a compound containing one oxygen atom bonded to two hydrogen atoms.

Supporting excerpt: "An oxygen hydride consisting of an oxygen atom that is covalently bonded to two hydrogen atoms"

## glycerol

Glycerol is a three-carbon alcohol with three hydroxyl groups.

Supporting excerpt: "A triol with a structure of propane substituted at positions 1, 2 and 3 by hydroxy groups."

Source: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/backend/api/public/compound/17754/

## citric acid

Citric acid is an organic acid with three carboxylic acid groups.

Supporting excerpt: "A tricarboxylic acid that is propane-1,2,3-tricarboxylic acid bearing a hydroxy substituent at position 2. It is an important metabolite in the pathway of all aerobic organisms."

Source: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/backend/api/public/compound/30769/

## ethanol

Ethanol is a primary alcohol built from a two-carbon chain with a hydroxyl group.

Supporting excerpt: "A primary alcohol that is ethane in which one of the hydrogens is substituted by a hydroxy group."

Source: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/backend/api/public/compound/16236/
